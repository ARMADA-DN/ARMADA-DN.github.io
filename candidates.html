Reliable Conversational Domain-specific

Doctoral Candidates

The ARMADA Doctoral Network brings together 15 highly skilled Early Stage Researchers from diverse backgrounds across Europe. These doctoral candidates are specializing in conversational AI, large language models, and reliable data exploration systems.
